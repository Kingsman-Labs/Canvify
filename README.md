## What It Does

Canvify turns a single sentence describing a startup idea into a complete,
investor-ready startup plan — autonomously. Eight AI agents validate the
idea, research the market, build a business model, project financials,
design a go-to-market strategy, and cross-check each other's work before
producing a final report. No manual research, no step-by-step instructions
— just an idea in, a full plan out.
